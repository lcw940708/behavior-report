Title: Leslie Cheung's 70th Birthday Memorial Exhibition Opens in September: A Glimpse into the Legend's Personal Treasures and Iconic Performances Date: September 3, 2026 HKT The much-anticipated memorial exhibition for Hong Kong icon Leslie Cheung (張國榮) is set to open on September 9, 2026, at the Hong Kong Cultural Centre. This grand celebration of the late singer's life and legacy will showcase over 300 precious items, some of which have never been displayed publicly before. Background: Leslie Cheung, a beloved figure in Hong Kong's pop culture history, would have turned 70 this year. To commemorate his life and artistic achievements, the Hong Kong Cultural Centre will present the "Continue to Cherish: Leslie Cheung 70th Birthday Memorial Exhibition" from September 9, 2026, to May 10, 2027. This event, organized by the Leisure and Cultural Services Department, promises to be a must-see for both veteran fans and newcomers alike, offering a unique opportunity to relive the iconic moments of "Brother" Cheung's illustrious career. Key Details: 1. Rekindling Personal Memories: The exhibition will feature an extensive collection of over 300 items, with a selection of previously unseen personal treasures. The event's curator, Cheung's former friend and manager, Susan Cheng, collaborated with Cheng's close friend and fellow artist, Tang Shu-wing, to lend his private collection for display. Highlights include the restoration of specific scenes from Cheung's former residence and the exhibition of his personal possessions and cherished photographs with friends. 2. Iconic Stage Costumes: Ten iconic costumes from different eras of Cheung's career will be on display, including the green jacket worn during his farewell concert at the Coliseum in 1989, as well as the gold outfit, pearl-embroidered blouse, and purple-striped pants from the "Crossing 97" concert in 1997. Each costume holds a valuable memory and adds to the excitement of revisiting Cheung's captivating performances. 3. A Cinematic Retrospective: The exhibition will also pay tribute to Cheung's impact on the film industry by showcasing six representative films throughout his career. From his debut in "Farewell My Concubine" as the youthful Chan Ho Nam to his role as the eccentric Zhang Wenjing in "The Maiden Phantom," fans will have the chance to reexperience the magic of Cheung's acting prowess on the big screen. Additionally, the exhibition will screen a special showing of "Crossing 97 Live Concert" at the end of the year, allowing fans old and new to feel the infectious energy of Cheung's live performances. Impact: - Exhibition Dates: September 9, 2026, to May 10, 2027 - Opening Hours: Tuesday to Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.; Saturday, Sunday, and Public Holidays, 10:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. (Closed on Mondays) - Location: Hong Kong Cultural Centre, 1/F, Special Exhibition Gallery 5 (1 Sha Tin Film Road, New Territories, Sha Tin) - Transportation: MTR Prince Edward Station Exit A, approximately 5-minute walk - Admission: Free Additional Attractions: "Kung Fu Theatre" brings art to life with a collection of Gu Long's Wuxia sculptures in the bustling marketplace. Summary: The "Continue to Cherish: Leslie Cheung 70th Birthday Memorial Exhibition" opens on September 9, 2026, at the Hong Kong Cultural Centre, showcasing over 300 precious items, including never-before-seen personal treasures and iconic stage costumes. The event aims to offer fans a unique opportunity to relive the unforgettable moments of Leslie Cheung's career, both in music and film. Bullet Points: 1.
