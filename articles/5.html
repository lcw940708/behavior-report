食安中心呼籲市民不要食用三批可能受李斯特菌污染的法國芝士 - Yahoo

發佈時間：Fri, 04 Sep 2026 12:18:23 GMT
段落1： Food and Environmental Hygiene Department (FEHD) urged the public not to consume three batches of French cheeses potentially contaminated with Listeria monocytogenes. FEHD received reports indicating that cheeses from Paturages Comtois, a French producer, may have been tainted. The affected batches are as follows: 3kg Brie with a 50% fat content from Noja Fine Trade Limited (Batch No: 00000BPBY1), 1kg Brie Grand Export from Maxly Food Company Limited (Batch No: 00000BXCG1), and 180g Pointe De Brie from King Rise (Asia) Limited (Batch No: 000000BXCG). 段落2: Listeria monocytogenes can survive and multiply at low temperatures, posing a risk to consumers. Most healthy individuals may exhibit mild symptoms such as fever, muscle aches, headaches, nausea, and diarrhea. However, infants, the elderly, and individuals with weakened immune systems are more susceptible to severe complications, including septicemia and meningitis, which can lead to death. Pregnant women are also at risk, as infections may result in miscarriage, stillbirth, preterm labor, or transmit the infection to the newborn. 段落3: The FEHD advised high-risk individuals, particularly pregnant women, to consume well-cooked hot foods and thoroughly reheat chilled food items. They were also advised to avoid high-risk foods like frozen meat products, chilled seafood, soft cheeses, and salads, or cook them thoroughly before consumption. FEHD instructed importers to halt sales and withdraw the affected batches from the market, and urged the industry to comply with these measures. 重點摘要： - 三批源自法國的芝士可能受到李斯特菌污染，食安中心呼籲市民不要食用。 - 高風險人群，包括孕婦、長者和免疫力較低的人，容易受到此細菌感染，可能引發嚴重疾病或死亡。 - 食安中心建議高風險人群避免進食高風險食品，或在進食前徹底熮熬或煮熟。
