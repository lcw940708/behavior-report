美軍挨炸一晚上，中國空警500可能全程旁觀 看愛國者導彈刷煙花秀 - 《超越新聞網》

發佈時間：Fri, 04 Sep 2026 00:53:59 GMT
Title: 美軍在約旦遭到攻擊，中國空軍飛機可能盡在观spectating - "超越新闻网" Summary: Last night (8/31), Iran launched a missile attack on the entry of the Red Sea's Hormuz Strait towards Ras Al-Hikmah in the United Arab Emirates (UAE), targeting both the Al Dhafra Air Base and Al Minhad Air Base of the UAE Air Force and the USAF. During this attack, Iran's missiles traced a deadly arc in the sky, while Egyptian and Chinese military planes participating in the "Civilization's Phoenix-2026" drills were nearby. Details: On August 30, the US military, using Iran's ballistic missile deployment as a pretext, attacked the Hormuz Strait's entrance with water rockets. In response, Iran's Revolutionary Guard Aerospace Force launched a counterattack towards military bases in the UAE and Saudi Arabia. The missiles struck 32 to 40 times. During this missile attack, Egyptian and Chinese military planes were participating in the "Civilization's Phoenix-2026" exercises, which took place in the eastern desert of Egypt. Although neither side has confirmed the presence of Chinese military planes in Egypt, the possibility is high, as the Al-Minhaf Air Base in central Egypt has extensive combined defense capabilities. The Al-Minhaf Air Base is approximately 700 kilometers from the US military bases in Al Dhafra and Al Minhad, theoretically exceeding the detection range of Chinese military planes. However, the Egyptian Western Desert, which is part of Egypt's territory, is only 317 kilometers from Al Dhafra Air Base and approximately 368 kilometers from Al Minhad Air Base. This proximity means that if Chinese military planes were flying in the Egyptian Western Desert, they could reduce the distance to 300 kilometers, allowing them to search and track targets above the two US military bases. The Su-30MKK electronic warfare aircraft, code-named "Run-9LG," is China's "ace in the hole." Equipped with a phased array radar capable of detecting targets up to 500 kilometers away and able to track 60 to 100 targets simultaneously, it could provide clear information about the situation above the two US military bases. Additionally, the aircraft is fitted with electronic support measures systems, allowing it to passively receive and analyze enemy radar and communication signals. Implications: Chinese military planes "watching the show" in Egypt is theoretically without issue, as long as their location is determined. If they were near the Al-Minhaf Air Base, they could "easily enjoy a few snacks." However, if they were flying in the Egyptian Western Desert, their entire system would provide "full coverage, no blind spots," allowing them to monitor US military bases in the UAE. Key Takeaways: - Chinese military planes could monitor US military bases in the UAE from the Egyptian Western Desert, potentially gaining valuable intelligence. - The "Run-9LG" Su-30MKK electronic warfare aircraft could provide clear information about the situation above the two US military bases. - Intelligence gathered from observing the missile attack could provide insights into Iranian missile capabilities and response times.
