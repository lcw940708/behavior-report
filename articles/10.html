Title: "Gundam: Crossing the Strange Journey" to be Released on March 5, 2027, with Customizable Mechs and Fast-paced Combat - 4gamers.com.tw The action game "Gundam: Crossing the Strange Journey" in the "Steel Ball Run" series will be released on March 5, 2027, for PS5/Xbox Series X|S/Steam. A "Sales Announcement Preview Video" was also unveiled, showcasing new gameplay footage. In addition to the revealed protagonist mech's upgrades, new characters and their mechs, powerful enemies named "Echoes," and an unknown black body were highlighted. Before engaging in combat, players can equip "Tactics Skills" in the customization menu for powerful instant attacks. Weapon choices are not the only customization feature. The game introduces a "Body Frame Customization System" consisting of five parts, each with its durability value and specific effects. The propulsion units differentiate between various movement characteristics, catering to horizontal or vertical movement types. The self-governing weapon pod "AP-U Support Unit (AP-U)" activates automatically and provides combat support with automatic shooting and deploying shields. The exterior can be freely colored in various parts, including the inner structure and glowing areas. Official modes also offer a "MOD Design" feature, allowing players to change the mech's appearance to classic models like the "RX-78-2 Gundam," without affecting weapon types or performance. The promotional video reveals three weapon types: "Assault Sword," which excels in charging powerful strikes and uses the exclusive skill "Anchor" to quickly close the distance to enemies; "Sword Lance," which specializes in mid-range thrust attacks and deals more damage the farther the target is hit, and can activate the special skill "Beam Barrage" for mid-range crowd control; and the "Whip Sword," which focuses on wide-area combat and can switch between sword and whip modes for closer combat and extended attack ranges. Pre-orders for both physical and digital versions are available starting immediately, with official announcements of collectible mech color schemes and MOD designs in the premium edition. Pre-purchasing the digital version or buying the physical version early grants the exclusive color "Earth Blue" for early unlock. Summary: - The "Gundam: Crossing the Strange Journey" game will release on March 5, 2027, for multiple platforms. - Players can customize mechs with "Tactics Skills," "Body Frame Customization System," and "AP-U Support Unit." - The game features three weapon types: "Assault Sword," "Sword Lance," and "Whip Sword." Key Points: 1. Customizable mechs with "Tactics Skills," a "Body Frame Customization System," and an "AP-U Support Unit." 2. Three distinct weapon types: "Assault Sword," "Sword Lance," and "Whip Sword." 3. Pre-orders for physical and digital versions include exclusive bonuses.
