最新天氣｜天文台：沙德爾減弱低壓區 取消所有熱帶氣旋警告信號 - 香港01

發佈時間：Thu, 03 Sep 2026 16:10:40 GMT
Segment 1: Tropical Storm Sidr Weakens, Cancels All Rainstorm Warnings in Hong Kong According to the Hong Kong Observatory (HKO), Tropical Storm Sidr is weakening and will cancel all rainstorm warnings. The storm is currently moving over Guangdong's eastern coastal areas and is expected to weaken into a low-pressure area. Once Sidr becomes a low-pressure area, the HKO will cancel all rainstorm warnings. As of midnight on September 3, all rainstorm warnings have been canceled. Segment 2: Sidr's Path and Impact on Hong Kong The HKO announced at 6:45 p.m. on September 3 that Sidr was moving over eastern Guangdong, near the Pearl River Delta, and was expected to weaken quickly. Strong winds were forecast for offshore areas and high ground in Hong Kong. The chances of changing the three-signal strong wind warning were low. The HKO also noted that Sidr's passage and the northeastern monsoon were influencing Hong Kong's weather, resulting in strong winds offshore and on high ground. Segment 3: Sidr's Future Impact on Hong Kong At 4:45 p.m., the HKO predicted that Sidr would be near the South China Sea's northeastern part and moving towards the western direction. It was expected to bring strong winds to offshore areas and high ground in Hong Kong, and the number one warning signal would be maintained. Sidr was forecast to be near the Pearl River Delta again, but its strength was uncertain. The HKO would closely monitor Sidr's movements and assess whether to change the three-signal strong wind warning when it approaches the Pearl River Delta. Bullet Points: - Tropical Storm Sidr is weakening and will cancel all rainstorm warnings in Hong Kong. - Strong winds are forecast for offshore areas and high ground in Hong Kong, but the chances of changing the three-signal strong wind warning are low. - The HKO will closely monitor Sidr's movements and assess whether to change the three-signal strong wind warning when it approaches the Pearl River Delta.
