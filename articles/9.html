TVB下周六播映特備節目悼念劉兆銘、紀念張國榮 重溫Ming Sir《倩女幽魂》姥姥經典角色 - 星島頭條

發佈時間：Fri, 04 Sep 2026 11:30:00 GMT
段落1： 香港傳奇表演人劉兆銘（Ming Sir）和巨星張國榮（James Wong) 2日（9月2日）離世， respective ages 94 and 70. Their passing left a deep impact on the local arts community and the general public. Plum TV (TVB) honors their memories with a special program on Sep 12 (Saturday), 8:30pm, featuring a remake of the classic TV series "The Resurrection of the Beautiful Demoness" (倩女幽魂) in which both stars appeared. 段落2: Plum TV pays tribute to the two legends by re-airing the classic TV series "The Resurrection of the Beautiful Demoness," in which they collaborated. The series, released in the 1980s, features Zhang's iconic performance as "Prince Ning" and Liu's memorable role as "Auntie the Black Tree Spirit." Liu's exceptional acting skills and avant-garde costumes brought the iconic villainous role to life, leaving an indelible mark on Hong Kong's entertainment industry. 段落3: Following the special program, Plum TV will air the original "The Resurrection of the Beautiful Demoness," starring Liu and Zhang. The film's release marked the height of their careers and remains a classic in Hong Kong's entertainment history. 重點摘要： 1. 劉兆銘和張國榮分別離世，Plum TV為他們重溫經典作品。 2. "The Resurrection of the Beautiful Demoness"是他們的經典作品，其中劉兆銘演藝出色。 3. 特別節目後續播映原片，紀念他們在香港影視界的珍貴貢獻。
