李卓耀時隔年半再闖750級別4強 下仗鬥「2哥」 - on.cc東網

發佈時間：Fri, 04 Sep 2026 04:28:15 GMT
Title: 李卓耀再闖入750級別4強，對手是「2哥」 - on.cc Eastern Daily News Li Cheuk Tung, a local badminton player, clinched the 4th seed spot in the 750-point league for the second time in six years on September 4, 2026, at 12:01 p.m. Background: Li, a 39-year-old athlete, qualified for the 4th seed in the Badminton World Federation's (BWF) Super Series event for the fifth time. Christo Popov, his rival, ranked 21st and 16th in the world. Key Details: - Li secured his place in the 4th seed after three rounds of qualifiers. - Li and Popov have faced each other eight times in the past. - The match between Li and Popov took place between rounds 15 and 21. Impact: - Li has won 37 out of 54 matches in the 4th seed. - Li's victory against Popov was his 21st win out of 40 encounters. - The success of Li in the 750-point league has boosted the morale of the local badminton community. Summary: Li Cheuk Tung, a local badminton player, qualified for the 4th seed in the BWF Super Series event for the fifth time. His main opponent was Christo Popov, ranked 21st and 16th in the world. Li secured his place in the 4th seed after three rounds of qualifiers. Li and Popov have faced each other eight times in the past, with Li winning 37 out of 54 matches and 21 out of 40 encounters.
