Title: 马天尼利转会希拉尔：阿仙奴出售最昂贵球员（Gabriel Martinelli from Arsenal to Al-Hilal for a Record-Breaking Transfer Fee） Background and Core Developments: Now Sports reported that Arsenal's Brazilian striker, Gabriel Martinelli, has joined Saudi Arabian club Al-Hilal in a historic transfer deal. The financial details of the transaction remain undisclosed, but according to reports, Al-Hilal paid around 6,000 million HKD for the 25-year-old player. This would break Arsenal's previous record transfer fee of 3,500 million HKD, which was paid to Liverpool in 2017 for Alexis Sanchez. Martinelli signed a four-year contract with Al-Hilal and expressed his excitement on social media: "I am proud to wear the blue jersey and to be a part of Al-Hilal." He made nearly 300 appearances for Arsenal, scoring 62 goals. Last season, he played in 53 matches and scored 11 goals, helping Arsenal regain the English Premier League title after a 22-year absence. Key Details and Data: With Martinelli's departure, he will join Al-Hilal's attack alongside Jonas Fernandes, who joined from Aston Villa for a reported 5,100 million HKD transfer fee. Now Sports also reported that Manchester United's Duje Caleta-Car was listed as a left-back in Manchester United's submitted 25-player squad for the English Premier League, indicating that the Danish international might take up a left-wing back position due to the team's shortage of defenders. However, Manchester United were unable to secure the signing of Nuno Mendes from Sporting Lisbon before the transfer window closed. Aftermath: Richarlison, who was rumored to join Manchester United, failed to register for the English Premier League and instead returned to Everton. The Brazilian striker had publicly expressed his desire to leave the club but ultimately remained a Toffee. The transfer window for the English Premier League closed on September 1, 2022. Bullet Points: - Gabriel Martinelli joined Al-Hilal for a record-breaking transfer fee of around 6,000 million HKD. - Manchester United listed Duje Caleta-Car as a left-back in their submitted squad, suggesting a potential new position for the Danish international. - Richarlison remained at Everton despite rumors of a move to Manchester United.
