Summary: Lee Chun-man, the lawmaker of Hong Kong's Democratic Party, was stripped of his legislative seat due to a disciplinary action. This came after he criticized the government's decision to suspend the electoral reforms. Details: On September 4, 2026, at 11:00 pm, Lee Chun-man was officially removed from his legislative seat following a disciplinary hearing. The Democratic Party member had been under fire for speaking against the government's decision to suspend the electoral reforms. The disciplinary committee decided to expel him based on Section 8(3)(b) of the Rules of Procedure of the Legislative Council. Impact: This decision marks the first time a legislator is removed from office due to a disciplinary action since the implementation of the National Security Law in Hong Kong. The incident has sparked controversy and debate among the public, with some expressing concern over the implications for freedom of speech and political plurality in the city. Key Points: - Lee Chun-man was stripped of his legislative seat due to disciplinary action. - The decision came after Lee criticized the government's decision to suspend electoral reforms. - This is the first time a legislator is removed from office due to disciplinary action since the National Security Law was implemented.
