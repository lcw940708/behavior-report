Title: 劉兆銘逝去，汪明荃分享珍貴影片致敬大師 At 18:00 on September 4, 2026 (HKT), the news broke that veteran actor Ming Sir (Li Kap-wong) of the Peony Rise Theatre passed away at the age of 94. Known for his significant contributions to the entertainment industry and his influential presence in both performing arts and choreography, Ming Sir was a pioneer in Hong Kong's dance scene, recognized as one of its pioneers. Last night (September 3), Ming Sir's disciple, Ming Sir Wang (Wong Ming-ho), shared a precious video on social media. The video featured a rehearsal session where Ming Sir personally instructed Ming Sir Wang in a dance routine, displaying his expertise and passion as a choreographer. Remarkably, Ming Sir was dressed in vintage work pants, showcasing his commitment to the art form and his dedication to teaching. Ming Sir Wang, then a young artist, wore a simple black shirt and blue trousers, focusing intently on the instructions and diligently following Ming Sir's lead. Key points: - Ming Sir, a renowned actor and choreographer, passed away at the age of 94. - Ming Sir Wang shared a video of a rehearsal session with Ming Sir, featuring Ming Sir's distinctive teaching style and vintage attire. - Ming Sir's influence and contributions to Hong Kong's dance scene are being recognized and celebrated. 1.
