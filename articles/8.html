Title: 東張西望｜淫窟中 female masseuse flees in panic after being exposed (Hong Kong 01) In the latest episode of TVB's investigative news program "东张西望" (East Meets West), the show uncovered a massage parlor in Mong Kok that had allegedly turned into a brothel. During the investigation, a TVB crew member was twice approached by female masseuses offering sexual services. Key Incident and Core Details: In the first instance, the female masseuse, upon realizing the crew member was from the program, quickly retreated into a room and closed the door, saying, "There's nothing here! There's nothing here!" She was wearing a clean outfit but had a red string thong hanging in the room. When asked why she was bathing in the shop, she replied, "It's hot today, I came back to work in the heat." After putting on her pants, she left the shop without a second thought. In the second instance, the crew member visited another massage parlor and identified himself to the staff. He received reports that the shop provided sexual services. The female masseuse, hearing the term "sexual massage," immediately entered a room and closed the door, but she couldn't escape the crew member's questioning. A man's voice was heard from an adjacent room, "Don't film me! Don't disturb me! I'll ruin my family!" The man, with his clothes covering his head, confronted the crew, demanding they stop recording. Aftermath: The female masseuse in the second incident seemed to have realized the crew was there to expose her shop, so she fled the scene. She made several rounds around the area before returning to her own shop. However, when the crew returned to the market, all the massage shops had already closed. Summary: 1. TVB's investigative news program "东张西望" uncovered a massage parlor in Mong Kok that had allegedly turned into a brothel. 2. Female masseuses approached the crew offering sexual services, and when their identities were revealed, they panicked and fled. 3. One male customer tried to prevent the crew from filming, fearing family repercussions. Bullet Points: - TVB's "东张西望" uncovered a brothel disguised as a massage parlor in Mong Kok. - Female masseuses offered sexual services to the crew and fled when discovered. - A male customer tried to prevent the crew from filming, fearing family consequences.
