2. 背景與概念解釋

房貸按揭計算（Mortgage Calculation） 係計算房屋貸款還款金額嘅財務工具。主要概念包括：

  • 貸款總額（Loan Amount）： 房屋總價減去首付金額後嘅實際借款金額。
  • 年利率（Interest Rate）： 銀行收取嘅年度貸款利率，直接影響每月供款金額。
  • 貸款年期（Loan Term）： 還款年期，通常為 20-30 年。
  • 等額本息還款法（Fixed-Rate Amortization）： 每月供款金額固定，但本金與利息嘅佔比會隨時間變化——初期利息佔比較高，後期本金佔比較高。

2. Background & Technical Concept

Mortgage calculation is a financial tool that calculates home loan repayment amounts. Key concepts include:

  • Loan Amount: The actual amount borrowed—home price minus down payment.
  • Interest Rate: The annual loan rate charged by the bank—directly affects monthly payments.
  • Loan Term: The repayment period, typically 20-30 years.
  • Fixed-Rate Amortization: Monthly payments stay fixed, but the principal-to-interest ratio changes over time—higher interest in early years, higher principal in later years.
