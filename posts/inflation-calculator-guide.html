2. 背景與概念解釋

通貨膨脹（Inflation） 係指物價水平持續上升、貨幣購買力相對下降嘅經濟現象。主要概念包括：

2. Background & Technical Concept

Inflation refers to the sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services, reducing the purchasing power of money. Key concepts include:

  • Purchasing Power: The quantity of goods and services a sum of money can buy. Higher inflation = lower purchasing power.
  • Cumulative Inflation: The total inflation percentage over a period of time.
  • Nominal vs. Real Value: Nominal is the future face value; real value is purchasing power adjusted for inflation.
  • Impact on Savings: If bank interest rates are lower than inflation, your savings' real purchasing power shrinks.
