2. 背景與概念解釋

72 法則（Rule of 72） 係一個經典嘅投資估算工具，用嚟快速計算複利效應下本金翻倍嘅時間。主要概念包括：

  • 72 法則公式： 翻倍年數 ≈ 72 ÷ 年化報酬率（%）。例如年回報 8% → 72 ÷ 8 = 9 年。
  • 點解係 72？ 因為 72 係一個「方便」嘅數字，可以被 2、3、4、6、8、9、12 整除，令心算變得好簡單。
  • 對數精確計算： 真正嘅複利公式係 翻倍年數 = ln(2) ÷ ln(1 + r)。72 法則只係一個「近似值」。
  • 反向應用： 如果你知道目標翻倍年數，可以用 72 法則反推所需報酬率：所需報酬率 ≈ 72 ÷ 目標年數。
  • 適用範圍： 72 法則喺 6% - 12% 嘅報酬率範圍內最準確，低於或高於呢個範圍會有誤差。

2. Background & Technical Concept

The Rule of 72 is a classic investment estimation tool for quickly calculating how long it takes to double your money under compound interest. Key concepts include:

  • Rule of 72 Formula: Years to Double ≈ 72 ÷ Annual Return Rate (%). For example, 8% annual return → 72 ÷ 8 = 9 years.
  • Why 72? Because 72 is a "convenient" number—it's divisible by 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, and 12, making mental math very easy.
  • Exact Logarithmic Calculation: The true compound interest formula is Years to Double = ln(2) ÷ ln(1 + r). The Rule of 72 is just an "approximation."
  • Reverse Application: If you know your target doubling years, you can estimate the required return rate: Required Rate ≈ 72 ÷ Target Years.
  • Accuracy Range: The Rule of 72 is most accurate for return rates between 6% - 12%. Outside this range, errors increase.
