2. Background & Technical Concept

Open Graph (OG) Tags is a protocol launched by Facebook in 2010 to enable richer content display on social media. The basic OG Tags include:

  • og:title — The title shown when shared
  • og:description — The description shown when shared
  • og:image — The thumbnail image shown when shared
  • og:url — The canonical URL of the page

Beyond OG Tags, there are Twitter Cards that control how your content appears on Twitter. Our tool generates both, ensuring consistent sharing across all major platforms.
