2. 背景與概念解釋

月平息（Monthly Flat Rate）與實際年利率（APR） 係借貸市場中最常用但又最容易混淆嘅兩個利率指標。主要概念包括：

  • 月平息（Monthly Flat Rate）： 按「原始貸款額」計算每月利息嘅方式。每月利息 = 貸款額 × 月平息率，無論你還咗幾多本金，利息都唔會減少。
  • 實際年利率（APR）： 採用內部回報率（IRR）計算，將所有利息、手續費、行政費及還款時間納入計算，反映真實年度借貸成本。
  • 點解 APR 會高過月平息？ 因為 APR 考慮咗「本金逐步償還」嘅因素——你每月還緊本金，但利息仍然按原始貸款額計算，所以實際利率比表面高。
  • 1.9 倍速算法則： 業界常用「月平息 × 12 個月 × 1.9」快速估算 APR。例如月平息 0.2% → APR ≈ 0.2 × 12 × 1.9 = 4.56%。
  • 手續費嘅影響： 一次性手續費會進一步推高 APR，因為你實際到手嘅資金少咗，但利息照舊按原額計算。

3. 實際應用場景

  • 財務健康檢查： 定期檢視現有貸款嘅實際利率，確保冇俾銀行賺多咗。

2. Background & Technical Concept

Monthly Flat Rate and Annual Percentage Rate (APR) are two of the most commonly used—but most easily confused—interest metrics in the lending market. Key concepts include:

  • Monthly Flat Rate: Interest is calculated on the "original loan amount" each month. Monthly interest = Loan Amount × Flat Rate, and it never decreases as you repay principal.
  • Annual Percentage Rate (APR): Calculated using Internal Rate of Return (IRR), factoring in all interest, fees, administrative charges, and repayment timing—reflecting the true annual cost of borrowing.
  • Why is APR higher than the flat rate? Because APR accounts for the "principal being paid down" factor—you repay principal each month, but interest stays at the original loan amount, so the effective rate is higher.
  • Rule of 1.9: A common industry rule of thumb: APR ≈ Monthly Flat Rate × 12 × 1.9. For example, 0.2% flat rate → APR ≈ 0.2 × 12 × 1.9 = 4.56%.
  • Impact of Fees: Upfront fees further increase APR because you receive less money upfront, but interest is still calculated on the original loan amount.
