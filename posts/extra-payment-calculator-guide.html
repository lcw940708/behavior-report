2. 背景與概念解釋

房貸提前還款（Extra Principal Payment / Mortgage Prepayment） 係指喺每月固定供款之外，額外償還貸款本金嘅財務操作。主要原理包括：

  • 利息計算基礎： 房貸利息係以「剩餘本金」計算嘅。還得越快，本金越少，利息就越少。
  • 額外還款（Extra Payment）： 每月額外償還嘅金額會直接扣減本金，而唔係用嚟支付未來嘅利息。
  • 複利效應反向操作： 原本銀行透過複利賺你錢，你提早還款就係 用複利嘅力量反過來幫自己慳錢。

2. Background & Technical Concept

Extra principal payments (mortgage prepayment) involve paying more than your scheduled monthly mortgage payment—the extra amount goes directly toward reducing your principal balance. Key concepts include:

  • Interest Calculation Basis: Mortgage interest is calculated on the "remaining principal." The faster you pay down principal, the less interest you accrue.
  • Extra Payments: Any amount you pay above the scheduled payment directly reduces your principal balance—not future interest.
  • Reverse Compounding: While banks use compounding to earn interest from you, extra payments use the power of compounding to save you money.

Our tool uses the standard fixed-rate amortization formula. All calculations are 100% client-side—your financial data never leaves your device.
